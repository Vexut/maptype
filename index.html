About

maptype is a free practice tool for learning the geography of the world by typing: countries and their capitals, plus the main rivers, seas, mountains and deserts. It works in many languages. Built by Vexut. © 2026 Vexut. All rights reserved, except for the third-party material listed under credits.

Privacy

maptype doesn’t collect any personal data. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or cookies, and the page doesn’t load anything from other websites.

Your settings (topic, difficulty, region, what to type, language and theme) are saved in your own browser’s local storage so they’re still there next time. They never leave your device. You can remove them by clearing this site’s data in your browser.

The service hosting this website may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses, to run and protect the site. Their own privacy policy explains how they handle that.

Questions or corrections:

Map and names

Country names are translated by your browser, so they can differ a little between browsers. Capitals, rivers, seas, mountains and deserts use translated names where they are available and fall back to English where they are not. English names are always accepted as an answer.

Borders come from Natural Earth (public domain) through the world-atlas project. They’re simplified for learning and don’t take a position on disputed borders or territories.

Names and capitals are checked carefully, but mistakes can happen. If your teacher’s material says something different, go with your teacher.
